Privacy Policy

App: Scan Doc Mate  |  Developer: Daynix Studio  |  Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Summary: Scan Doc Mate is a fully offline Android application. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information. All scanned documents and PDFs remain exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Scan Doc Mate does not collect any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, or analytics. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use its features.

2. Camera Permission

The app requests access to your device's camera for one purpose only: capturing images of physical documents for scanning. Camera data is processed entirely on your device. No images are uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external server.

3. Local Storage

All scanned documents, PDFs, thumbnails, and application settings are stored exclusively on your device's internal storage. This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to the app itself.

4. Internet Access

Scan Doc Mate functions completely offline. The app does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or third-party services.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google's on-device ML Kit Document Scanner for edge detection and image enhancement. All processing happens locally on your device. No document data is transmitted to Google or any other third party.

6. Advertisements

Scan Doc Mate contains no advertisements, no advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.

7. Analytics and Tracking

The app contains no analytics tools, crash reporting services, or user tracking of any kind.

8. Children's Privacy

Scan Doc Mate is suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, no data is collected from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

9. Data Security

Because all data remains on your device, the security of your scanned documents depends on your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device secured.

10. Your Control Over Data

  • Delete any document or folder from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all scanned documents and PDFs stored by it.
  • You have full control — no data recovery service is offered because no data is stored outside your device.

11. Permissions Used

  • Camera — to capture documents for scanning.
  • Storage — to save generated PDFs on your device.

12. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published at the same URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Developer

This app is developed by Daynix Studio. Visit us at daynixstudio.com.
